2.3 Data stored locally on your device

To throttle the optional donation prompt — so it does not appear too often — the app stores a small number of values in Android's SharedPreferences, which is private application storage isolated to this app on your device:

  • donation.calc_count — total number of calculations performed (integer).
  • donation.calc_count_at_last_prompt — value of the counter at the time the donation prompt was last shown (integer).
  • donation.last_prompt_ts — timestamp (in milliseconds since the Unix epoch) of the last donation prompt.
  • donation.rejection_count — how many times the donation prompt was dismissed (integer).
  • donation.has_donated — boolean flag indicating whether at least one successful donation has been made via Google Play.

These values never leave your device. They contain no personal data, no identifiers, and no information about what you calculated. They are deleted automatically when you uninstall the app or when you clear the app's data via Android system settings.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in not over-prompting our users with donation requests). You can object at any time by clearing app data; the app remains fully functional.

2.4 Android permissions used

  • android.permission.WAKE_LOCK — used solely to keep the device screen on while you have the calculator open, so the app remains usable beside your monitor during gameplay. No data is collected through this permission.

The app does not request internet, location, storage, contacts, camera, microphone, Bluetooth, or any other sensitive permission.

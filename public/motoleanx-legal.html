2. Freiwillige Übermittlung anonymisierter Fahrdaten

2.1 Wie die Übermittlung ausgelöst wird

  • Einmalige Einwilligung: Sie werden einmal gefragt und stimmen durch aktives Betätigen der Schaltfläche „Ja, ich helfe mit“ zu. Danach werden Aufzeichnungen ab einer Länge von 10 Minuten automatisch übermittelt. Kürzere Aufzeichnungen bleiben auf Ihrem Gerät.
  • Einzelne Fahrten: Unabhängig davon können Sie jede einzelne Aufzeichnung über das Menü in der Fahrtenliste manuell teilen.
  • Rohe Sensordaten: Solange die automatische Übermittlung eingeschaltet ist, zeichnet die App zusätzlich die rohen Messwerte von Beschleunigungssensor und Gyroskop auf. Übertragen wird davon höchstens der Ausschnitt der ersten 15 Minuten einer Fahrt. Diese Werte enthalten keinerlei Ortsbezug; sie sind das, woran sich verbesserte Auswerteverfahren nachrechnen lassen.
  • Widerruf: Sie können die automatische Übermittlung jederzeit in den Einstellungen der App abschalten. Damit endet zugleich die Aufzeichnung der rohen Sensordaten. Der Widerruf wirkt für die Zukunft.

2. Voluntary Transmission of Anonymised Ride Data

You may make recorded rides available to us so we can improve lean angle measurement. This is entirely voluntary; the App works exactly the same without it.

2.1 How transmission is triggered

  • One-time consent: you are asked once and consent by actively tapping “Yes, I will help”. After that, recordings of 10 minutes or longer are transmitted automatically. Shorter recordings stay on your device.
  • Individual rides: independently of this, you can share any single recording manually from the menu in the ride list.
  • Raw sensor readings: while automatic transmission is switched on, the App additionally records the raw values from the accelerometer and gyroscope. At most the first 15 minutes of a ride are transmitted from these. The values contain no location reference whatsoever; they are what allows improved algorithms to be verified against real rides.
  • Withdrawal: you can switch automatic transmission off at any time in the App settings. This also ends the recording of raw sensor readings. Withdrawal takes effect for the future.

TransmittedNever transmitted

  • device model, manufacturer, chipset, Android version, memory, screen resolution

The cornering rate is calculated on your device from consecutive positions; the positions are then discarded. Only the result is transmitted, never the underlying coordinates.

2.3 Anonymity and residual risk

With location and time removed and without any identifier, the transmitted dataset can no longer be attributed to a specific person. It therefore constitutes anonymous data within the meaning of Recital 26 GDPR.

For completeness we note a theoretical residual risk: speed and cornering rate allow the shape of a route to be reconstructed geometrically. That shape contains no location reference—there is no starting point and no compass direction. Where you rode cannot be determined from it.

2.4 Legal basis

  • Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR—your consent, insofar as any personal data (such as the IP address during the connection) is processed in the course of transmission.
  • Section 25(1) TDDDG (German implementation of the ePrivacy Directive)—your consent for accessing information stored on your terminal device (recordings and technical device/sensor details). This consent is required regardless of whether the data is personal.

2.5 Upload code and deletion

Every transmitted ride receives a random upload code (example: KSC9-6RVR). You will find all your codes in the App settings. Because we deliberately store nothing that points back to you, this code is the only way to identify a specific transmission: send it to us by email and we will delete that dataset.

2.6 Storage location and retention

Transmitted data is stored on a server operated by us in Germany (IONOS SE, data centre in the EU). It is not passed on to third parties. Transmitted data is automatically and permanently deleted from the server no later than 90 days after receipt. Independently of this, you can request early deletion of a specific contribution at any time via the upload code (section 2.5).
